Chess Lens Live

Watches a real board through your camera, follows the moves as they're played, and shows the best move with an arrow.

  1. Start from the starting position, or tap Set up current position later to enter a game already in progress.
  2. Prop your phone so it sees the whole board and doesn't move. Higher and more overhead is better.
  3. Tap the board's 4 corners on screen.
  4. Play. After each move, take your hand away for a second so it can see the board.
